## 🎯 **How the AI Conversation Works**

### **Multi-Turn Conversations**

The AI remembers context and can have natural back-and-forth:

```
You: "I need to update an employee"

AI: "Sure! Which employee would you like to update?"

You: "Sarah Johnson"

AI: "Found Sarah Johnson in Sales. What would you like to update?"

You: "Her salary"

AI: "What should Sarah's new salary be?"

You: "$85,000"

AI: "Current: $75,000
     New: $85,000
     
     ⚠️ This requires a reason for audit compliance.
     Why are you making this change?"

You: "Annual performance review - exceeded targets"

AI: "Preview:
     Employee: Sarah Johnson
     Field: Salary
     Current: $75,000
     New: $85,000
     Reason: Annual performance review - exceeded targets
     
     Shall I apply this change?"

You: "Yes"

     📋 Change recorded in audit log"
```

## 🔐 **Safety & Compliance**

### **Built-In Safeguards**

✅ **Audit Logging**
- Every change recorded
- Who made it
- What changed
- Why it changed
- When it happened

✅ **Preview & Confirmation**
- See changes before they happen
- Must explicitly confirm
- Can cancel at any time

✅ **Reason Requirements**
- Changing existing data requires explanation
- Ensures compliance
- Creates paper trail

✅ **Undo Capability**
- Reverse changes within 48 hours
- Restores previous values
- Also audit logged

✅ **Admin-Only Access**
- Currently restricted to admin users
- Full permission system integration
- Company-scoped data access

✅ **Rate Limiting**
- 500 requests per hour
- Prevents accidental overuse
- Protects OpenAI costs

## ❓ **Common Questions**

### **"What if I make a mistake?"**
Use the undo feature within 48 hours. Just say "Undo that change" and the AI will reverse it.

### **"Can I see what changed?"**
Yes! All changes are in the audit log. Navigate to the employee profile → Audit History.

### **"What if the AI doesn't understand?"**
Rephrase your question. The AI learns from different ways of asking. You can also click the suggested examples.

### **"Is my data safe?"**
Absolutely. The AI:
- Only accesses your company's data
- Requires admin authentication
- All actions audit logged
- OpenAI doesn't store your data permanently
- Built on your existing security model

### **"Can I export AI interactions?"**
Yes, all AI actions are recorded in system audit logs and can be exported.

### **"What fields can be updated?"**
Ask the AI: "What fields can you update?" and it will provide a complete list.
