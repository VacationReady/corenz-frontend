# Journey Designer AI Knowledge Base

## 🎯 **AI Assistant Journey Designer Capabilities**

The AI Assistant has been fully integrated with comprehensive Journey Designer knowledge and can handle all aspects of employee experience orchestration through natural conversation.

### **Core Journey Design Actions**

#### 1. **Journey Creation** (`create_journey`)
- **Natural Language**: "Create an onboarding journey for software engineers"
- **AI Response**: Generates complete journey template with phases, experience blocks, and success metrics
- **Features**: Persona-based templates, industry best practices, customizable phases

#### 2. **Journey Optimization** (`optimize_journey`) 
- **Natural Language**: "Optimize the onboarding journey performance"
- **AI Response**: Analyzes completion rates, satisfaction scores, and suggests improvements
- **Features**: Performance analytics, bottleneck identification, A/B test recommendations

#### 3. **Experience Block Management** (`add_experience_block`)
- **Natural Language**: "Add a survey after the welcome email"
- **AI Response**: Inserts appropriate experience blocks with proper sequencing
- **Block Types**: Welcome emails, training modules, surveys, check-ins, document collection

#### 4. **A/B Experiment Design** (`create_experiment`)
- **Natural Language**: "Test welcome email timing for new hires"
- **AI Response**: Creates experiment configuration with traffic splits and success metrics
- **Features**: Statistical significance tracking, variant management, performance comparison

#### 5. **Performance Analysis** (`analyze_performance`)
- **Natural Language**: "How is the onboarding journey performing?"
- **AI Response**: Provides completion rates, satisfaction scores, and trend analysis
- **Metrics**: Engagement rates, time-to-complete, satisfaction scores, drop-off points

#### 6. **Content Generation** (`generate_content`)
- **Natural Language**: "Write a welcome email for new software engineers"
- **AI Response**: Generates personalized, role-specific content
- **Content Types**: Emails, survey questions, training materials, check-in prompts

#### 7. **Decision Gateway Logic** (`add_decision_gateway`)
- **Natural Language**: "Route based on department after onboarding survey"
- **AI Response**: Creates conditional logic with department-specific paths
- **Features**: Rule-based routing, conditional workflows, personalized experiences

#### 8. **Improvement Suggestions** (`suggest_improvements`)
- **Natural Language**: "What can be improved in our employee experience?"
- **AI Response**: AI-powered recommendations based on data analysis and best practices
- **Sources**: Performance data, industry benchmarks, employee feedback
