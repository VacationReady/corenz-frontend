## 💡 Pro Tips

### Make the AI Work for You
1. **Be conversational**: "Give sales a raise" (not "bulk update sales salaries")
2. **Use follow-ups**: "Their emails?" instead of "Show me sales team emails"
3. **Drag and drop**: Faster than navigating menus
4. **Trust previews**: They show exactly what will happen
5. **Undo freely**: 48-hour safety net

### Smart Commands
```
✅ "What's our total payroll?"
✅ "Who doesn't have IRD numbers?"
✅ "Give IT a 5% raise"
✅ "Show me upcoming contract renewals"
✅ "Upload this to Sarah"
✅ "Email all managers about the policy change"
```

### Avoid These
❌ "Increase salaryAmount by 10 percent for departmentId sales"
   → Too technical, just say "Give sales a 10% raise"

❌ Asking the same question twice without "undo"
   → Say "undo that" first, then make the change again

❌ Uploading without employee name
   → AI will ask, but faster to say upfront

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### "No employees found in [department]"
**Fix**: Check department spelling. Try "Show me all departments"

### "Bulk update failed"
**Fix**: Check if employees have salaries set. Missing data can cause failures.

### "Can't scroll up"
**Fix**: Should be fixed now! If not, refresh the page.

### "Wrong employee count"
**Fix**: Should be fixed! AI now uses conversation context correctly.

### "Undo not working"
**Fix**: Must undo within 48 hours. After that, changes are permanent.
